# Code Of Conduct

Treat collaborators respectfully regardless of background or experience. Discuss evidence and code, not personal attributes. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and intentional falsification are unacceptable.

Respect privacy and attribution. Do not post identifying experimental/clinical data or confidential security details. Clarify uncertainty and credit original investigators and providers.

Maintainer Berfin Nida Öztürk may remove harmful contributions, request corrections or restrict participation. Raise conduct concerns privately through an established owner contact. A dedicated reporting channel must be configured before opening external contributions; no monitored email is claimed here.
